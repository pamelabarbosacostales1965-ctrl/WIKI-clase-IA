## Study route

1. Explain a rational agent and build a PEAS description.
2. Formulate a problem with initial state, actions, transition, goal, and cost.
3. Trace BFS, DFS, greedy best-first, and A*; state their assumptions.
4. Evaluate minimax and identify alpha–beta cutoffs.
5. Model a CSP and explain early pruning.
6. Translate logic into Prolog; distinguish truth from successful proof search.
7. Compare numerical and population-based optimization.
8. Calculate one step of GD, SA, PSO, ACO, and ABC.
